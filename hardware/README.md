# CAD, assembly and bill of materials

**Pending the final STL files and assembly information.** The simulations and numerical studies are available now; the mechanical package will be completed separately.

Planned contents:

- `stl/`: individual 3D printed parts with clear names, units and printing notes.
- `cad/`: any available native assemblies or exchange models.
- `animations/`: rotating assembly view and demonstrations of actuator movement.
- `assembly/`: a full CTR exploded view with the actuator kept in place, plus a detailed exploded view of one carriage showing its motor, chuck, motor holder, optical end stops and physical stoppers.
- `bill-of-materials.csv`: verified part names, descriptions/specifications, quantities and file or supplier references.

CAD screenshots can establish placement and assembly order. Separate part files and movement information are needed to produce accurate exploded views and motion. STL meshes alone do not establish units, assembly relationships, hardware specifications or counts.

No bill of materials is supplied yet because the complete parts and quantities have not been verified.
